# Preliminary audit checklist

- [ ] Confirm exact facility/building and geographic scope.
- [ ] Identify issuer, publication date, access date, and relevant page/section for every source.
- [ ] Civil/structural: record available design basis, geotechnical report, loads, drawings, and signed reviews; do not infer absence from a limited search.
- [ ] Environmental: locate applicable approval/AMDAL documents and confirm facility identity, issuing authority, dates, and scope.
- [ ] Electrical: record utility connection, redundancy claims, single-line diagrams, UPS/generator data, and testing evidence when publicly available.
- [ ] Mechanical: record cooling topology, capacity, water use, refrigerants, and commissioning evidence where documented.
- [ ] IT: distinguish marketing claims from measured availability, capacity, security, and network evidence.
- [ ] Regulatory: map each requirement to the applicable jurisdiction, date, and primary legal source.
- [ ] Mark unknowns explicitly; never turn a search gap into a failure finding.
