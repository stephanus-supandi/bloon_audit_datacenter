# Scope

BLOON_AUDIT_DATACENTER performs preliminary document-based screening across civil/structural, environmental, electrical, mechanical, IT, and regulatory domains. It validates data shape, tracks source references, and produces reproducible CSV and Markdown outputs.

It does not certify safety, determine legal compliance, authenticate documents, inspect a site, or replace qualified engineers, environmental consultants, regulators, or legal counsel. Conclusions must remain proportional to available evidence.
